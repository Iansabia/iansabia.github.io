01 / Research · DS340

Complexity is not an edge

Polymarket and Kalshi often price the same event differently. Does a fancier model profit more from those gaps? No. The simplest model won, with about 30× more edge per trade.

02 / Research · DS480

Does a name change what an AI agent buys?

AI agents are starting to shop for people. I built a mock store and ran a pre-registered audit: 65,280 runs of the same requests on Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna, with only the buyer's name changed. Luna paid less for names associated with Black buyers and with women, and guessed hair needs from names. Haiku showed no price gap.
