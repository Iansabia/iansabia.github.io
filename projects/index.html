SwapChain

A room-swap marketplace for BU, built in my product management class with a team of four. BU's swap tools only match pairs. SwapChain finds loops of students who can all move at once (A takes B's room, B takes C's, C takes A's). I built the demo: a 3D campus map, floor plans and the loop finder. The film follows a swap list of 350 students, all made up. No sound.

Team of 4 · 3D campus map · 2-min film

Continuum

An AI caddy that runs on your phone. It tracks your shots from ordinary video, gives swing feedback, and tells you where to aim based on where you've been missing. About 70 conversations with golfers moved us from indoor simulators onto the course. I lead product and ML. The demo plays hole 10 at Miacomet Golf Club on Nantucket in 3D. The golfer and the shots are made up. The course is real.

~70 golfer conversations · 0.91 F1 ball tracking

03 / Work · NVIDIA challenge

Teaching a car to explain itself

NVIDIA's Physical AI challenge asks a self-driving model to explain why the car makes its decision in unusual scenes. I took NVIDIA's own driving model, Alpamayo 1.5, trained it further on the task, and got it running on a MacBook, 4 to 7× faster. In the demo you make the call on four real dashcam clips, then see what my model said.

10B parameters · 4–7× faster answers · $0 spent
