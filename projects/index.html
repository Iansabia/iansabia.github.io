Product · DS229 team projectFilm · 2 min

SwapChain

A room-swap marketplace for BU, built in my product management class with a team of four. BU's swap tools only match pairs. SwapChain finds loops of students who can all move at once (A takes B's room, B takes C's, C takes A's). I built the demo: a 3D campus map, floor plans and the loop finder. The film follows a swap list of 350 students, all made up. No sound.
