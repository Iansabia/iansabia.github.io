SwapChain

A room-swap marketplace for BU that finds loops of students who can all move at once, not just pairs. A team project from my product management class. I built the demo.

Team4Ideas scored12My partThe demoBuilt withClaude Code

The problem

01

We believe first-year BU students with an incompatible roommate are frustrated by getting out of a room that isn't working, because a swap only happens if the student with the room they want also wants theirs.

“The number one reason my residents swap is due to roommate incompatibility.”

A BU RA on our team
2Max students in a BU swap
~350Students on a volunteer swap list
1,000+In a BU housing Discord

Why loops

02

Think Secret Santa. You hand your room to the next person and get the room of the person before you.

Student ATakes B's room

B's room is the one A wants.

Student BTakes C's room

B doesn't want A's room, so a pair swap never happens.

Student CTakes A's room

C wants A's room, which closes the loop.

How the loop finder works. Every posted room is a node, with an arrow from A to B when A would accept B's room. A depth-first search lists every loop of up to 5 students, pruned by a breadth-first distance check. Then a branch-and-bound search picks the set of non-overlapping loops that moves the most students. It's the same cycle-packing problem kidney exchanges solve, and like them it prefers short loops, because one person backing out breaks the whole loop.

BU only processes 2-person swaps, so a loop of k students is filed as k−1 back-to-back BU Direct Swaps. Nothing is filed until every person in the loop confirms.

How we got there

03

The product work behind the demo, from the class's framework.

  1. 01
    Personas and pain pointsTeam

    Four personas. For our main one, a first-year with a bad roommate, we ranked pain points by urgency × frequency × impact. Roommate incompatibility scored highest.

  2. 02
    12 ideas, scoredTeam

    Each idea got 1 to 5 on impact, feasibility, differentiation and cost. SwapChain scored 16 of 20. Every other option, from BU's own tool to the Discord, matches one pair at a time.

  3. 03
    PRD, OKRs and the MVPTeam

    MVP must-haves: post a room, the loop finder, confirm first, and roommate profiles. The riskiest assumption: will students post? Our key results are targets, not results.

  4. 04
    The working demoMe

    A 3D campus map, floor-by-floor plans showing who's leaving and who's staying, room posting, and the loop finder (DFS cycle search + branch-and-bound cycle packing). Built with Claude Code in plain JavaScript and deck.gl.
