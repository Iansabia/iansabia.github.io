Continuum is an AI golf caddy that tracks your shots from ordinary phone video and tells you where to aim, based on where you've been missing. After the shot, it helps you find your ball. I co-founded it and lead product and ML.

02 / The pivot

The pivot

We changed what we built after about 70 conversations with golfers.

BeforeIndoor simulators

We started out building software for indoor simulators. I had done the product research and thought we had a great solution.

What changedAbout 70 conversations

Playing on the course and talking with about 70 golfers led my co-founder and me to pivot.

AfterOn the course, on the phone

We first framed it as Strava for golf. Today it is an AI caddy. Running on the phone kept it free, with no launch monitor or hardware to buy.

The lesson I took from it: when the evidence points somewhere else, keep your ego out of it.

03 / How we test it

How we test it

We test everything against hand labels before we trust it.

What I built. I lead product and ML. From June to September 2026 I built our perception stack from scratch, the software that sees the ball and the swing.

My ball tracker was a neural network (a U-Net) in PyTorch that read 5 frames at a time and marked where the ball was. I built it from four published methods, with no pretrained weights.

On October 1, 2026 we ran rented AI models, ones we pay to use, on our own footage. On the same 91 hand labeled frames, SAM 3.1, a general purpose vision model, found the ball in flight better than our tracker, so we moved ball detection to it. F1 is an accuracy score where 1.0 is perfect. It is one comparison, not a final score.

65Real shots in our ground truth label set
7,500Frames in it, with a note of where each label came from

How we keep score. Before training our own models, I froze a test split and kept it out of training. In our reports, every metric comes with a 95% confidence interval and is judged against pass or fail targets we call release gates. We now use those gates to grade rented models against human labels.

04 / How we got there

How we got there

From indoor simulators to a caddy on your phone, and who did what.

  1. 01
    Talked to golfersTeam

    About 70 conversations moved us from indoor simulator software to an AI caddy for the course.

  2. 02
    Built our own perception stackMe

    June to September 2026. A ball tracker built from scratch, a strike timer that listens to the audio, and a swing finder that follows the body with MediaPipe pose.

  3. 03
    Tested it against rented modelsMe

    October 1, 2026. SAM 3.1 beat our own tracker on the same hand labeled frames, so we moved ball detection to it.

  4. 04
    Where it stands nowTeam

    We are building a shot detection pipeline from SAM 3.1, pose models and a vision language model that acts as judge. Any disagreement goes to a human. The target is a 100% correct shot list on five range sessions, judged on human labels plus two videos we keep sealed and run only once. We have not run them yet, so there is no final score.
